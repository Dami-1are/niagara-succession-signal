# Processed Data

This folder holds cleaned Week 4 outputs kept separate from the raw source files.

These files are NOT final analytical datasets. No integrated or modelling-ready table exists yet. Datasets at different geographic levels (Niagara, Ontario, Canada) must not be merged without a documented, valid join.

Current contents:
- statcan/business_dynamics/ : Business Dynamics by industry and by firm size, two separate files (Ontario, contextual evidence)
- cfib/ : structured CFIB succession survey evidence (Canada, contextual evidence)

Other cleaned Week 4 outputs are in data/interim/. See docs/WEEK4_HANDOVER.md for cleaning decisions and limitations.
