# Data Licences and Usage

This document records the licence and usage status of the main data and research sources identified for the Niagara Succession Signal project.

Licence conditions will be checked again when datasets are downloaded in Week 3.

---

## 1. Statistics Canada

**Licence:** Statistics Canada Open Licence

**Status:** Open for use

The Statistics Canada Open Licence allows us to use, reproduce, publish and distribute Statistics Canada information and to create value-added products from it.

### Project Rule

Whenever Statistics Canada information is used, we will:

- acknowledge Statistics Canada as the source;
- identify the relevant table/product and reference period;
- reproduce the information accurately;
- avoid suggesting that Statistics Canada endorses our project;
- avoid misrepresenting the information or its source; and
- comply with the conditions of the Statistics Canada Open Licence.

This applies to the Statistics Canada sources selected for this project, subject to checking for any identified third-party material.

**Official licence:**
https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/terms-conditions/open-licence

---

## 2. Niagara Region Open Data

**Licence:** Open Government Licence 2.0 — Niagara Region

**Status:** Open for use

Information covered by this licence may be copied, modified, published, adapted and distributed for lawful purposes, including commercial purposes.

### Required Attribution

Where applicable, we will acknowledge the source using the attribution required by Niagara Region.

A suitable attribution is:

> Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Niagara Region.

### Project Rule

For Niagara Open Data datasets we will:

- record the dataset name;
- preserve the source link;
- record the date downloaded;
- record the applicable licence;
- provide attribution;
- avoid suggesting Niagara Region endorses our project; and
- check the individual dataset page before use to confirm that the stated licence applies.

**Official licence:**
https://niagaraopendata.ca/pages/open-government-license-2-0-niagara-region

---

## 3. Consolidated Niagara Employment Inventory

**Provider:** Niagara Region

**Source:** Niagara Open Data

The Consolidated NEI dataset is obtained through the Niagara Open Data catalogue.

Before using the downloaded dataset, we will confirm and record the licence shown on its individual dataset page.

If it is covered by the Open Government Licence 2.0 — Niagara Region, we will follow the attribution and usage requirements described above.

### Project Rule

The original downloaded dataset will be preserved unchanged in `data/raw/`.

---

## 4. Niagara Economic Development

**Status:** Supporting/reference material

Niagara Economic Development website content is not being treated in this project as an unrestricted open dataset.

Its website terms permit certain non-commercial uses of text subject to stated conditions, while images, photographs and other media may have separate copyright restrictions.

### Project Rule

We will:

- use relevant figures and information as supporting evidence where permitted;
- cite the original webpage/report;
- record the reference year;
- avoid copying entire webpages, reports, photographs or other protected material into the repository;
- avoid assuming that website images or media can be reused; and
- check the terms applying to any specific downloadable dataset or report before redistributing it.

---

## 5. CFIB Succession Research

**Provider:** Canadian Federation of Independent Business (CFIB)

**Status:** Research/reference material

CFIB research is copyrighted material.

CFIB's terms allow reproduction for purposes including research, private study and education, subject to its terms.

### Project Rule

We will use CFIB succession research as supporting evidence and national context.

We will:

- cite CFIB as the source;
- cite the specific report;
- paraphrase findings where appropriate;
- avoid uploading or redistributing the complete CFIB report as though it were our own dataset; and
- avoid presenting CFIB national findings as Niagara-specific findings.

---

# Repository Rule

Public availability does not automatically mean unrestricted reuse.

Before any new external dataset is added to the project, we will record:

1. Source organisation
2. Dataset/report name
3. Official source link
4. Licence or terms of use
5. Download/access date
6. Attribution requirement
7. Redistribution restrictions, if any

If the licence cannot be identified, we will not assume that the data can be redistributed.

---

# Attribution Rule

Every dataset used in the final project must be traceable back to its original source.

Our analysis, charts, tables, methodology and final reports will clearly identify the sources used.

Where we transform an open dataset, we will make it clear that the resulting analysis or model is our team's work and does not represent endorsement by the original data provider.
